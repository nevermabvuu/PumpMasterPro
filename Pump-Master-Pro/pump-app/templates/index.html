NFPA 20, NFPA 13, and EN 12845 life safety compliance. Automated churn pressure limits, 150% flow overload margins, and fire water tank sizing.

Slurry Derating View Module →

PumpMasterPro slurry derating calculating HR, QR, ER correction factors based on solids concentration Cv and particle size d50, plus ANSI/HI viscous fluid curves.

Pipe Network View Module →

Hydraulic modeling for series and parallel piping networks using Darcy-Weisbach friction loss, Colebrook-White friction factors, static elevation, and fitting K-values.

Curve Fitting View Module →

Polynomial regression curve fitting for H-Q head, Efficiency (η), Power consumption (kW), NPSHr, affinity-law impeller trims, and closed iso-efficiency contours.
