Typical Fire Pump Performance Curve (NFPA 20 Requirements)

Characteristic operating points, hydraulic zones, and code limit boundaries.

Letters [A–G] designate key curve points without clutter
Key NFPA 20 Requirements
  • E Shutoff Head Limit: Shutoff (churn) head must NOT exceed 140% of rated head (prevents pipe/fittings overpressure).
  • F Rated Capacity: At 100% rated capacity, pump must provide at least 100% of rated head.
  • G 150% Overload Capacity: At 150% rated flow, pump head must NOT fall below 65% of rated head.
Operating Regions
  • I 0% – Churn / Closed Discharge: High pressure, zero flow. Requires casing relief valve to prevent heat buildup.
  • II ~75%–110% – Normal Rated Operating Area: Certified fire demand envelope where system sprinkler demand operates.
  • III 100%–150% – High-Flow / Overload Region: Check maximum system demand, available suction NPSHa, and non-overloading driver power.
Key Operating Points

  • B Rated Duty Point: 100% flow @ 100% head (Certified Design Duty).
  • C System Operating Point: Intersection of pump curve and hydraulically most remote/favourable system curve.
