Selection Tool

Displays superimposed Head (H-Q), Efficiency (η-Q), Power (P-Q), and NPSHr curves. Automatically calculates proximity to Best Efficiency Point (BEP) and indicates Preferred Operating Regions (POR).

CAD Designer

Full orthogonal pipe routing with integrated elbows, reducers, and inline valves. Computes Darcy-Weisbach head loss using Colebrook-White friction factors and overlays system curves onto pump curves.

Easily add new pump models with live interactive curve fitting. Enter manufacturer test bench points, define impeller trim diameters, specify motor ratings, and preview fitted curves in real-time.
