Additional Pipe Analysis Calculations Included:
  • Particle Settling Velocity (Vt): Universal explicit formula (Ferguson & Church 2004) covering laminar Stokes to turbulent Newton regimes.
  • Durand Critical Deposition Velocity (Vc): Prevents bed deposition (sanding hazard) by alerting if pipeline velocity V < 1.2 Vc.
  • Joukowsky Water Hammer Analysis: Composite wave speed a = √(K_f / ρ / (1 + (K_f/E)(D/t))) and surge pressure ΔP = ρ · a · ΔV.
  • Barlow Pipe Hoop Stress (σ): σ = (P · OD) / (2t) with safety factor vs material allowable yield stress.
